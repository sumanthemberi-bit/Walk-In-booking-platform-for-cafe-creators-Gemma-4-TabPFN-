# Evaluation

How Walk-In's two open models were measured. Everything here is generated by `make_eval_reports.py` from the result files in this folder; nothing is typed in by hand.

## 1. Best slots: TabPFN vs simple averages

**Question:** given a creator's own small log of sittings, does TabPFN predict a sitting's earnings better than the rule of thumb a creator would use?

**Method:** walk forward through the log in date order. Each sitting after the first 8 is predicted using only the sittings before it, then compared with what actually happened. Script: `evaluate_slots.py`.

| Predictor | Average error per sitting |
|---|---|
| TabPFN v2 | ₹1,439 |
| Per-cafe weekday/weekend averages | ₹1,554 |
| Overall mean of earlier sittings | ₹3,352 |

TabPFN was closer than the averages on **4 of 6** predicted sittings.

| Date | Cafe | Actual | TabPFN | Averages | Overall mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-06 | Janu | ₹7,220 | ₹7,162 | ₹6,970 | ₹10,066 |
| 2026-10-07 | Blondie House | ₹6,109 | ₹6,653 | ₹5,553 | ₹9,750 |
| 2026-10-09 | Blondie Khar | ₹9,996 | ₹9,646 | ₹8,516 | ₹9,386 |
| 2026-10-10 | Blondie Khar | ₹17,771 | ₹14,211 | ₹13,325 | ₹9,441 |
| 2026-10-11 | Janu | ₹13,329 | ₹11,770 | ₹12,218 | ₹10,135 |
| 2026-10-13 | Blondie Khar | ₹8,887 | ₹6,322 | ₹7,405 | ₹10,381 |

**Limits:** 6 predictions from a small log, so this is a sanity check, not proof. `sample_sittings.csv` is made-up test data with real cafe names. Rerun on a real log as it grows.

## 2. Messages: does Gemma follow the rules?

**Question:** how often does a draft break the rules a creator cares about, and what does each part of the prompt and the checker add?

**Method:** 10 real situations (thank-yous after hard readings, booking confirmations, gentle nudges, payment reminders) × English, Hinglish and Hindi = 30 drafts per version, each checked automatically by `guard.py`. Script: `evaluate_messages.py`. Every draft is in `eval_messages.csv`.

Checks: **length**: 2 to 4 sentences; **emoji**: at most one emoji; **hashtags**: no hashtags; **price**: no ₹ amount that wasn't given; **time**: no clock time that wasn't given; **private**: doesn't copy 4+ words from the private session notes; **language**: right language and script (Hindi in Devanagari, Hinglish in Latin letters with Hindi words).

| Prompt version | Passed every check | length | emoji | hashtags | price | time | private | language | Avg seconds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 plain request | 0/30 | 30 | 30 | 28 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 15 | 45.9 |
| v2 + rules | 27/30 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 10.7 |
| v3 + rules + voice | 26/30 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 4 | 10.1 |
| v4 + checker, one retry (shipped) | 29/30 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 12.7 |

Numbers under each check are drafts that failed it (a draft can fail several).

In the shipped version, **8 of 30** drafts needed the one retry.
