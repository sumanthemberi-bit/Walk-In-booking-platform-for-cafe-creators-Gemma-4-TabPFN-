# Walk-In: review from a tarot reader

One of Walk-In's first testers reads tarot and takes bookings through Instagram and WhatsApp. Their review, unedited:

> As someone who does tarot readings, I think Walk-In is a genuinely useful idea, especially for people like me who take bookings through Instagram or WhatsApp.
>
> One thing I liked is that customers can see what services I offer, how much they cost, and when I'm available, all in one place. Usually, I have to explain these details to every person who messages me, so having a booking page would make things much easier.
>
> The QR code feature is also something I can see myself using at cafés or small events. People could just scan it, check my sessions, and book without having to message me first.
>
> I also liked the idea of having AI help with follow-up messages. It could save time, although I'd still want to edit the messages to make sure they sound like me.
>
> One thing I'd suggest adding is an easier way for customers to reschedule their appointments, since plans can change at the last minute.
>
> Overall, I think Walk-In solves a real problem for independent artists and readers. It's a simple concept, but I can see it being helpful for someone trying to manage a small side hustle alongside their regular work.

**What happened next:** AI drafts are always edited and sent by the creator, never sent automatically. Rescheduling is on the list: today a customer cancels and rebooks from their booking link, or messages the creator on WhatsApp.
