Two sides, one Walk-In

Customers and creators see different things. Customers never need an account.

Customers see

  • Your public page: photos, services, prices, where you sit
  • Only the times you've opened, so no double bookings
  • A simple 3-step booking: service, time, name and WhatsApp
  • A private link to check or cancel their booking

You see: Creator studio

  • Bookings inbox: confirm or decline with one tap
  • Your schedule: weekly sittings, one-off dates, days off
  • Messages and posts drafted for you, in your voice
  • Your sittings log and Best slots predictions

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Walk-In doesn't charge creators or customers, and never takes a cut. Customers pay you directly.

Do my customers need an app or an account?

No. They open your page, pick a time and enter their name and WhatsApp number. That's it.

Does the AI send messages for me?

Never. It writes a draft. You read it, change anything you like, and tap Send in your own WhatsApp.

Where are my clients' notes stored?

On the Walk-In server, visible only to you. The AI runs on that same machine, so notes aren't sent to OpenAI, Google or any AI company. You can download or delete everything under My data.

Do I need a laptop?

No. The Creator studio works on your phone, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.
